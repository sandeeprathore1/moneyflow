## Overview

MoneyFlow ("we", "our") is a personal finance app that helps you track expenses, budgets, and financial goals.

## Data We Collect

- **Account data:** Email address and password (hashed) for authentication
- **Financial data:** Transactions, categories, budgets, and goals you create or confirm
- **Notification data (Android only):** Payment notifications are parsed **on your device**. We receive only structured fields (amount, merchant, date) — never raw notification text.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- SMS messages
- Contacts or call logs
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers

## How We Use Data

- Provide expense tracking, budgeting, and analytics
- Detect duplicate transactions
- Generate spending insights and optional AI assistant responses

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Optional AI features may send anonymized financial summaries to OpenAI when you use the assistant and an API key is configured by the service operator.

## Data Retention & Deletion

You may export your data (JSON/CSV) or delete your account from the app. Account deletion permanently removes your data from our servers.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Access tokens expire regularly.
